How it works

From today's trips to a signed statement

Four steps, the same way every single day — however many terminals or operators you run.

1

Enter

Import today's trip manifest from Excel, or key it in manually with built-in validation.

2

Calculate

Deductions, additions, and commission are applied automatically to every line.

3

Reconcile

Terminal and operator figures are compared, and any mismatch is flagged right away.

4

Settle

Once confirmed, a PDF statement is generated, archived, and ready to send.
